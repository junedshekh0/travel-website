FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a package?

Send an enquiry using the form above or call us. Our trip manager will share a final quote and you can confirm by paying a 25% advance.

Can I customise a package?

Yes. You can change hotels, add or remove days and include extra activities. Choose "Custom Trip" in the form and tell us what you need.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 15 or more days before travel get a full refund minus a small processing fee. Later cancellations are charged based on hotel and flight policies.

Are flights included in the price?

International packages include flights where mentioned. For domestic packages, flights or trains can be added on request.

Do you help with visas?

Yes, we assist with visa documentation and applications for all our international packages.
