Our Story

We plan trips the way we'd plan our own

Safarnama started in 2015 as a small team of friends in Jaipur who loved travelling and were tired of confusing packages with hidden costs.

Today we help thousands of travellers every year, but the idea is still the same: clear prices, good hotels and someone you can actually call when you need help.

  • Every hotel is personally verified by our team
  • One fixed price with no hidden charges
  • A dedicated trip manager for every booking
  • Easy cancellation and date change
