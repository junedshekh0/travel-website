Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Safarnama ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use our website or book a trip with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to plan and manage your trip:

  • Contact details such as your name, email address and phone number, when you send an enquiry or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Trip details such as the package you choose, travel dates, number of travellers and any preferences you share with us.
  • Traveller documents such as ID or passport details, only when they are required for hotel check-in, permits, flights or visas.
  • Payment information needed to process your booking. Card details are handled by our payment partner and are not stored by us.
  • Usage data such as pages visited, browser type and device, collected automatically to help us improve the website.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and send you quotes.
  • To make and manage bookings for hotels, transport, activities and visas.
  • To send booking confirmations, itineraries and important travel updates.
  • To send offers and newsletters, only if you have subscribed.
  • To improve our website, packages and customer service.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed to deliver your trip or when the law requires it:

  • Travel partners such as hotels, airlines, transport operators and local guides, who receive only the details needed for your booking.
  • Service providers such as payment gateways, email and hosting providers who work on our behalf.
  • Authorities, where required for visas, permits or by law.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the site may not work as well without them.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep enquiry and booking records for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal and accounting obligations. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your information, where we are not required by law to keep it.
  • Stop sending you marketing messages at any time.

8. Third-party links

Our website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

9. Children

Our services are meant for adults. Bookings that include children must be made by a parent or guardian, who provides the child's details on their behalf.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the date it was last updated.

11. Contact us
